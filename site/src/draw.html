What a drawing is

A list of shapes. Never code, never a picture the model painted.

Everything on the sheet is a box, an ellipse, a line, an arrow, a piece of text, or one of your strokes. That list is what Fogar saves, what the model reads, and what it has to send back.

Straight talk

Diagrams, not art.

The rest of Fogar is a new tab that answers from a model running in your browser. See what leaves in each mode.

  • The model draws boxes, ellipses, lines, arrows, and text. It cannot make freehand strokes, so “draw a cat” gets you a cat made of ellipses.
  • Small models often miss the format. On the 0.8B and 2B device models, expect “The model did not answer with shapes” more often than a drawing. The 4B, or a cloud model, follows it.
  • With a cloud model, the shape list and your instruction go to that provider, because that is where the model is. On the device, nothing leaves.
  • One sheet per widget, a fixed size. No infinite canvas, no layers, no sharing, no sync.
Questions

Fair questions, short answers.

Which models can draw?

Any model that answers with the shape list Fogar asks for. In practice that is the Qwen3.5 4B on this device or a cloud model such as Claude, GPT, or Gemini through your own key. The smaller device models often answer with prose instead; when that happens the canvas stays as it was and the widget says so.

Does my drawing leave my computer?

Not with the device model. With a cloud model, the list of shapes goes as text to the endpoint you chose, along with your instruction. Your freehand strokes are described only by an id and a bounding box, never their points.

What if the model wrecks my drawing?

Press undo. A model’s reply is a single undo step, and the last sixty changes are kept. The model also cannot move or redraw your own strokes: it can keep them, recolor them, or leave them out.

How do I get the drawing out?

Save PNG, in the card’s corner, downloads the sheet at 1600 by 1000 pixels on a paper background. The drawing itself also rides along in Fogar’s backup file.

Can I draw with a pen or tablet?

The canvas listens to pointer events, so a mouse, a trackpad, a touch screen, or a stylus all draw. Pressure is not used; stroke width comes from the width menu.
